# Learner guide

Welcome to Praxis. This guide walks you through the whole loop: **learn** in courses, **practise** with
quizzes, **simulate** real work, **perform** in practical assignments, **get assessed**, and **improve** with a
personalised plan.

Everything you need is under **Learn** in the sidebar. Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**) to search from anywhere.

> **Try it with the demo data.** Sign in as `learner@northwind.demo` (Alex Chen, the main demo learner) with
> the password `Praxis-Demo-2026!`. Alex has courses in progress, quiz and simulation results, a certificate and a streak.

## 1. Your dashboard

![Learner dashboard](images/learner-dashboard.png)

The **Dashboard** shows:

- a **Resume** button that takes you to where you left off, and **Ask the AI tutor**
- your **weekly goal** ring and your current and best **streak**
- tiles for courses in progress, courses completed, certificates, streak, average score and time learned this week
- **Continue learning** cards with progress and due dates
- **Upcoming deadlines** for the next 30 days
- **Recommended for you**: personalised next steps based on your results and skill gaps
- your recent simulation results, skills, certificates and activity

You can change your weekly learning goal in **Profile**.

## 2. Find and enrol in courses

1. Open **Catalog**.
2. Search courses, topics or skills, and filter by category, difficulty, skill or enrolment (All courses, My courses, Not enrolled).
3. Open a course to see what you'll learn, who it's for, prerequisites, the outline, the skills you'll build,
   the instructor, and whether it awards a **verifiable certificate**.
4. Select **Enrol**, then **Start learning**.

Your organisation may also assign courses to you with a due date. They appear without you having to enrol.

## 3. My learning and learning paths

**My learning** has four tabs:

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **In progress** | Courses you've started, with progress |
| **Assigned** | Training your manager or organisation assigned, with due dates |
| **Completed** | Finished courses |
| **Learning paths** | Programmes made up of several courses in order. Select **Join path** to enrol in its published courses. |

**Course versions.** When you enrol, you stay on the version of the course you started, so your progress
and scores never change underneath you. If the instructor publishes a new version, you may see **Upgrade
course**, which moves you to the latest version.

## 4. Take a lesson in the course player

1. Open a course and select a lesson from the outline on the left. Modules show how far through them you are.
2. Lessons can be reading, video, audio, PDF, slides, images, interactive widgets, code walkthroughs,
   flashcards, branching scenarios, AI-tutor practice, or a linked quiz, assignment or simulation.
   Some lessons include **knowledge checks**, quick ungraded questions.
3. Praxis tracks your time and position as you go, so you can pick up where you left off.
4. If the course uses **sequential lessons**, later lessons unlock once you complete the required ones before them.
5. Use the focus toggle in the top bar to hide distractions.

When you complete every required lesson (and pass the graded work), you see a completion celebration. If the
course awards one, your certificate is issued automatically.

## 5. Ask the AI tutor

![Lesson with the AI tutor panel open](images/course-player-tutor.png)

- In a lesson, select **Ask AI tutor**. The tutor knows which course and lesson you're on. Use **Full
  screen**, or open **AI Tutor** in the sidebar, for longer conversations. Your past conversations are kept there.
- Try the starter prompts ("Explain this lesson in simpler terms", "Give me a real-world example", "Quiz me on
  the key points", "What mistakes do people usually make here?") or ask your own question.
- Answers are grounded in your course and your organisation's knowledge base, and list their **Sources**.
  Rate answers with thumbs up or down. AI can make mistakes, so check the cited sources.

**Hint mode during graded work.** While you have graded work in progress for the course (an open quiz attempt, an
assignment draft or a running simulation), the tutor **gives hints, not answers**. It won't reveal correct
options, model answers or simulation root causes. That's by design, so your results reflect what you know.

> In the demo, the tutor runs on an **offline demo model** and says so in a banner. Answers are drawn
> from the course materials, not from a general-purpose AI.

## 6. Take a quiz or assessment

1. Open the quiz from its lesson. The start screen shows the instructions, number of questions, pass mark,
   time limit, attempts allowed, your best score and previous attempts. **High stakes** assessments are labelled.
2. Select **Start assessment**.
3. Answer the questions. Depending on the type, you might choose options, type a short or long answer, match
   pairs, drag items into order, fill in blanks, write code, upload a file, work through a scenario or decision
   tree, or pick one of your completed simulation sessions to be scored.
4. Your answers **save automatically**. Use the **question navigator** to move around and **Flag for review**
   to come back to a question. A timer shows when the assessment is timed. When time's up, your saved
   answers are submitted for you.
5. Select **Submit**.

Your result shows your score and whether you passed. Depending on the assessment's settings, you'll also see
correct answers and explanations, either straight away or once your attempts are used up. Long answers,
files and code may say **Awaiting review** until an assessor marks them.

## 7. Complete a practical assignment

**Assignments** in the sidebar lists your practical work. Filter it by **To do**, **In review**, **Graded** or **All**.

1. Open an assignment and read the brief: the situation, objective, instructions, resources, and **How
   you'll be assessed** (rubric weighting).
2. The **Evidence checklist** shows what you must submit, such as a written report, screenshot, code or structured answers.
3. Write your response (Markdown supported, with **Write** and **Preview** modes), attach files and fill in
   any structured fields. Files are virus-scanned (when scanning is configured) and stored privately, so only you and your assessors can open them.
4. Select **Save draft** as often as you like. Every save is kept in your **version history**.
5. Select **Submit for assessment** once every required item is in place. Your submission is locked while it's assessed.

### Feedback

- If your organisation uses AI-recommended grading, you may first see **AI recommendation — awaiting
  assessor**. Your final score and written feedback appear once an assessor confirms or adjusts it.
- Feedback shows your level on each rubric criterion, plus strengths, things to improve, missing requirements and next steps.
- **Returned for changes**: your assessor wants a revision. Edit and resubmit.
- **Not passed this time**: you can try again if attempts remain.
- **Think something was missed?** Select **Request a human review** and say which criterion and which evidence the reviewer should look at.

## 8. Run a simulation

Simulations put you in a realistic work environment, such as a service-desk ticket, a phishing investigation or
a customer complaint. Talk to people, use tools and make decisions. Every action has consequences.

1. Open **Simulations**, filter by industry or difficulty, and open one to read its briefing.
2. Select **Start simulation**, then **Enter the workspace**.

![Simulation workspace](images/simulation-workspace.png)

3. In the workspace you'll find:
   - the **phase tracker** (Initial → Investigation → Diagnosis → Remediation → Validation → Resolution)
     and a hint about what to do next
   - **tools** in tabs, such as the ticket, a remote PowerShell terminal (type `help`), system
     information, a knowledge base and a directory admin console
   - **chat** with the people involved. Their mood (for example "Calm") reacts to how you treat them.
   - the simulated **clock** against the target time, the **Actions** menu, and **Finish**
4. Select **Finish** when you've resolved the issue. A session you leave stays in progress, so you can open it again from **Simulations**.

### Results and bands

![Simulation debrief](images/simulation-results.png)

Your debrief shows a score out of 100, your **band** and whether you passed:

| Band | Score |
|---|---|
| Expert | 95+ |
| Advanced | 85–94 |
| Competent | 70–84 |
| Needs Improvement | 50–69 |
| Fail | below 50 |

These are the default bands and pass mark (70). A simulation can define its own. Taking a critical wrong action
caps your score. You'll also see:

- **Performance by dimension** (for example technical accuracy, troubleshooting process, communication,
  decision making, efficiency)
- **Coach's debrief**: strengths, things to improve, missed critical steps and tips
- **Action replay** of every step, with **Mistakes only** and **Play replay** options

Select **Try again** if you have attempts left, or **Ask the AI tutor** to talk through what happened.

## 9. Certificates and verification

When you complete a course that awards a certificate, it's issued automatically and shown under **Certificates**.

- **Download PDF**: a branded certificate with a QR code.
- **Copy link**: share your public verification page.
- Anyone can check a certificate at `/verify/<certificate number>` (numbers look like `CERT-2026-000111`), or
  by scanning the QR code. The page shows whether the certificate is **verified**, **revoked** or **expired**,
  along with the recipient, course, organisation, issue date, score and the skills demonstrated.

**Certificates within reach** lists courses you're close to finishing.

## 10. Skills and recommendations

**Skills** is your competency profile:

- **Proficiency by skill** against the 70% competence line, with levels from Emerging and Developing up through
  Competent and Advanced to Expert
- **Competency attainment**, if your organisation defines competencies (groups of skills with target levels)
- **Weak areas**: error rate by skill, your most-missed questions and topics to revisit
- **Recommended next**: lessons to review, practice, simulations to retry and a weekly **revision plan**,
  based on your weakest skills and recent mistakes. You can dismiss a recommendation to tune future suggestions.

Quizzes, assignments and simulations all add evidence to the skills they cover.

## 11. Achievements

If your organisation has turned on gamification, **Achievements** shows:

- **points** and your **level** (Novice → Apprentice → Explorer → Practitioner → Specialist → Expert →
  Master → Mentor → Champion → Legend). For example, a completed lesson earns 10 points, a passed quiz 50, a passed assignment 75 and a completed course 200, and simulations earn points by band.
- your **streak** and **badges** (locked badges show what you need to do to earn them)
- a **Leaderboard** for this week, this month or all time, for the whole organisation or by department

Prefer not to appear on the leaderboard? Select **Hide me from the leaderboard**. You still earn points and badges privately.

## 12. Profile, security, MFA and privacy

Open your avatar menu, then **Profile**.

### Profile

Full name, avatar image URL, language, **time zone** (your streaks and activity days use it) and **weekly learning goal**.

### Security & sign-in

1. **Password**: change your password. Use a long, unique one. Other devices may be signed out.
2. **Two-step verification (MFA)**:
   1. Select **Set up two-step verification**.
   2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (1Password, Authy, Google Authenticator and so on), or copy the setup key.
   3. Enter the 6-digit code.
   4. **Save your recovery codes**. Each one works once if you lose your device.

   If your organisation requires MFA, you'll be asked to set it up and won't be able to turn it off.
3. **Sessions & devices**: see where you're signed in and revoke any session you don't recognise, or sign out all other devices.

### Privacy & data

- **Consent preferences**: AI-powered features, product analytics, and news and tips by email. Every change is
  recorded in your consent history.
- **Download your data**: request a ZIP (JSON) of your profile, enrolments, progress, assessments,
  submissions, simulations, certificates and AI conversations. It can be downloaded for 7 days.
- **Delete account**: permanently deletes your account and personal data after you confirm with your password.
  Your account is anonymised rather than erased row by row, so training records, certificates and the audit
  trail stay consistent without your personal details.

Your organisation may also delete old notifications and AI tutor conversations automatically under its data
retention policy.
